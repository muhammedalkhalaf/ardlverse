# Derivation ledger: `hausman_test()` (ardlverse 2.1.2, R/panel_ardl.R lines 801-922; helper `.check_hausman_compatible()` lines 928-961)

Scope: the Hausman (1978) test of long-run slope homogeneity, MG (inefficient, consistent) versus PMG or DFE (efficient under H0). Inputs are the `long_run`, `long_run_vcov`, `sigma` and `ec_group` components of `panel_ardl()` objects (R/panel_ardl.R lines 228-586, unchanged since 2.1.0). This ledger is the audit ledger of 2.1.1 (audit of 2026-10-30) with the code lines and the verification column updated to the 2.1.2 code. Tests named below are in tests/testthat/test-panel_ardl.R.

Paper text: Hausman (1978), Econometrica 46(6), 1251-1271 (Econometrica page numbers); PSS (1999) = Pesaran, Shin and Smith, Pooled mean group estimation of dynamic heterogeneous panels, discussion-paper page numbers in brackets. Stata sources read during the audit: `hausman.ado` version 3.2.7 (14dec2022) and `xtpmg.ado` version 2.1.1 (SSC, 06jul2026; original Blackburne and Frank 1.1.1); the [R] hausman manual PDF.
